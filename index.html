Welcome to the Tiger's Den

A community built on growth, on and off the court

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Do you have scholarships?

We have sponsors who love supporting individual athletes that they believe are upcoming talent. Athletes are encouraged to find sponsorship within their circles of influence, as not all can be given a scholarship through our program, but can find funding through a personal sponsor.

How long is this program?

This is an at-will program, allowing your athlete to leave when they wish, but for best outcome, a full 10-month season or full calendar year are recommended to ensure your athlete gets the opportunity they signed up for.

Who is the team staff who will be mentoring my child?

Take a moment and get to know each of our staff. Handpicked for skill, adaptability, and caring personalities — each of our staff finds pride in helping grow tomorrow's future.

Where does TAP train and play?

Our home base is Swysh Den Basketball, 8212 E Evans Rd, Scottsdale, AZ 85260 — Scottsdale's premier indoor training facility, offering weight room training, jump training, Vertimax, and recovery tools.
