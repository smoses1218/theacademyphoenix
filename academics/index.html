Basketball & Education

Academics

The Academy Phoenix offers two distinct educational pathways for its high school and post-graduate student-athletes.

Post-Graduate Program

Justice University

We partner with Justice University to offer college-level courses, helping our post-grad student-athletes gain a head start on their academic careers. Student-athletes live off-campus in a Team House only 10 minutes from Justice University, and practice, compete, and attend classes directly at the university's brick-and-mortar campus.

High School Program

TAP Elite High School Prep

High school students can fulfill all their graduation requirements through our exclusive TAP Elite Prep School — accelerating athletic development while seamlessly continuing their high school education.

International Assist Initiative

Support for International & National Athletes

TAP partners with International Assist Initiative (IAI) to offer comprehensive services, ensuring that international and national students have a seamless and successful experience as they embark on their academic and athletic journeys in the U.S.
